title: Body Battery
description: "A simple 0 to 100 daily energy score."
---

Body Battery tells you how much energy you have today, relative to **your own** recent baseline. It's a wellness indicator, not a medical measure.

## Formula

The score starts at **75**. Each contributor adds or removes points, then the total is clamped to 0 to 100.

| Contributor | Compared to | Points | Range |
|-------------|-------------|--------|-------|
| Sleep | Average of the previous 13 nights | `(today − avg) × 6` per hour | −20 to +15 |
| HRV (SDNN) | Average of the previous 13 days | `(today − avg) / avg × 50` | −20 to +15 |
| Resting heart rate | Average of the previous 13 days | `(avg − today) × 2` per bpm | −15 to +10 |

Lower resting heart rate and higher HRV than usual both add energy.

## Source

`apps/web/src/lib/energy.ts` exports `computeEnergy(days)`, which returns the score, each contributor's value, baseline, and impact, plus today's and yesterday's data.

<Note>
  This is a deliberately simplified daily version for the demo. It is not a continuous, minute-by-minute model.
</Note>
