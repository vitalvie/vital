Vital's site is a product-led landing page. The working demo is the hero; the sections below it tell the story. The look sits in the same family as modern French health products: warm cream, pastel blocks, large soft shapes and big, tight headlines. The identity is Vital's own.

## Principles

1. **The product is the hero.** A visitor can talk to Vital within three seconds. The watch on screen is the real control, never a mockup.
2. **One main action per screen.** Talk to Vital. Everything else supports it, and details come on demand.
3. **Calm and soft.** Pastel blocks, very large radii, diffuse shadows. No hard borders, no pure black.
4. **Honest data, honest proof.** Always show today's number next to the usual one. Never diagnose. No testimonials or invented figures.
5. **Motion explains.** It shows a state change or where to look next. It never decorates.

Each section owns one pastel. Neighbors never share one.

### Type

Font: **Alan Sans**, weights 400, 500 and 700. Self-hosted as one variable file (`apps/web/public/fonts/alan-sans-latin.woff2`, latin subset, SIL Open Font License), preloaded, `font-display: swap`.

The scale is fluid between 360 and 1440 px. Headlines are medium weight, large and tight.

| Class | Size | Use |
|-------|------|-----|
| `text-display` | 44 to 80 px, line-height 1.02, tracking -0.035em | The `h1` and the final call to action |
| `text-title` | 32 to 56 px, line-height 1.08 | Section titles (`h2`) |
| `text-subtitle` | 22 to 28 px, line-height 1.2 | Card and step titles (`h3`) |
| `text-lead` | 17 to 20 px, line-height 1.5 | One lead sentence under a title |
| Body | 16/24, `paragraph` | Everything else |
| Label | `text-sm` or `text-xs`, `caption` | Labels, status, legal |

Metrics use `tabular-nums` so counters don't shift.

### Orb and watch states

| Status | Orb | Icon | Watch shows | Tap does |
|--------|-----|------|-------------|----------|
| `idle` | Slow breathing, a ring drifting outward | Mic | "Tap to talk" | Start recording |
| `listening` | Bright halo, scales with live mic volume | Arrow up | "Listening… tap to send", mic meter, cancel | Send the recording |
| `thinking` | Fast spinning halo | Three dots | The question, in quotes | Nothing |
| `speaking` | Shrinks to the bottom of the screen | Stop | The answer, large, with cancel | Stop playback |
| `idle`, after an answer | Stays small at the bottom | Mic | The answer stays readable | Start a new question |

A long answer scrolls inside the watch screen and fades out at the bottom. The same answer is always in the conversation panel.

### Body Battery

The level pill, the gauge and the dot on the watch share one color per level: `bad` for Low, `warn` for Moderate, `good` for Good.

Each contributor shows today's value, its unit, the gap to the usual value (`+0.7 h`, `−3 bpm`) and the usual value. The gap pill is colored by what it does to the score, not by its sign: a lower resting heart rate is green. Contributors are rows in a narrow card and tiles in a wide one, switched by a container query.

### Notices

When Vital can't listen or can't answer, a `peach` notice shows a short title, one line of help and at most one action. It appears under the hero questions and in the conversation panel; only the latter announces itself. Copy lives in `src/lib/notice.ts`. Keep it warm and free of technical words.

| Case | Title | Action |
|------|-------|--------|
| Page is not secure | Mic needs HTTPS | Open secure link |
| Browser can't record | Voice isn't available here | Type instead |
| Permission denied | The mic is switched off | Type instead |
| Nothing transcribed | I didn't catch that | None |
| Empty answer | I don't have an answer for that one | Type instead |
| Network down | I can't reach Vital right now | Try again |
| Any other failure | Something went wrong on my side | Try again |

When voice is unavailable the orb dims, shows a crossed mic, and a tap moves focus to the text input. If only the spoken reply fails, the written answer stays and no notice shows. While thinking, a three-line skeleton holds the answer's place.

## Page structure

```
Nav             wordmark · anchors · GitHub · Try it          sticky
Hero            headline + lead │ live watch on a pastel stage
                suggested questions │ Body Battery preview
Demo            indigo block: watch (sticky) │ demo control, Body Battery, conversation
How it works    three pastel cards: Ask, Understand, Answer
Benefits        bento grid, four tiles
Safety          teal block: evals │ four guardrails
Under the hood  night block: five-stage pipeline
Open source     peach block: licence, GitHub, release, CI
Final CTA       indigo block
Footer          wordmark, medical disclaimer, links
```

Below `lg` everything is one column. In the hero the order is headline, watch, Body Battery preview, questions, so the orb sits in the thumb zone. The watch is drawn at full size and zoomed down per placement (`[zoom:…]` classes passed to `Watch`), so layout follows its real size.

From `lg` the hero has two columns, with the Body Battery preview floating over the edge of the stage, and the demo watch is sticky beside its panels.

## Motion rules

- Animate `transform` and `opacity` only. Colors may cross-fade.
- The hero enters in order: eyebrow, lead, watch, questions. The `h1` does not animate, so it paints first.
- Content below the fold is wrapped in `Reveal`. Markup is visible by default; only what starts off screen is hidden after hydration, then fades up once.
- Numbers count to their new value (`useCountUp`), the gauge slides with `translateX`, history bars grow with `scaleY`, the segmented control's thumb slides.
- A global rule in `styles.css` ends every animation and transition at once under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility rules

- One `h1`. Each section is a `<section aria-labelledby>` with an `h2`. Cards use `h3`.
- A "Skip to the demo" link is the first tab stop.
- The focus ring is global (`:focus-visible` in `styles.css`): indigo, white on the watch screen. Don't restyle it per component.
- Every control has an accessible name. Decorative visuals are `aria-hidden`.
- The answer is announced from one `aria-live` region in the conversation panel. The copy on the watch is hidden from assistive tech to avoid a double reading.
- Text contrast meets WCAG AA on every block, including `night` and `indigo-500`.

## Rules

- Reuse tokens, shared classes and components before adding new ones.
- Keep copy short, warm and non-clinical. Suggest a professional when something looks concerning. No medical claim, no promised result.
- Sample data is always labeled ("Demo data", "Runs on sample data").
- Check layouts at 360, 390, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 px with no horizontal scroll. Grid tracks use `minmax(0, 1fr)` so content can shrink.
- No dark mode yet. The pastels and the watch are tuned for the cream background.
- No UI library. Tailwind and plain React only.
